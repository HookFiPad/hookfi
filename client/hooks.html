Quote through the hook

Name two tokens. Every indexed pool for the pair is read, the deepest are quoted through their hooks, and the routes are ranked.

Routes appear here. Each one is quoted by the on-chain V4Quoter, so the pool's hook actually runs. The swap is built unsigned: your wallet signs it, and nothing here ever holds funds.

Mine a hook address

Pick the permissions your hook needs. The miner finds a CREATE2 salt whose address ends in exactly those 14 bits.

The salt is only valid for the exact init code you mine it for: the creation code plus its ABI-encoded constructor arguments. Deploy with new Hook{salt: salt}(…) from a Foundry script and the address will match.
